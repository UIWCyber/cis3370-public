A Log Line Is Just Fields

Pulling one field out of an auth log
$ awk '{print $1, $3, $9}' auth.log# print columns 1, 3 and 9
09:14:22 sshd Failed
$ awk '$9=="Failed" {c++} END {print c}' auth.log# count the failures
418
  • No database. No dashboard. One line, one answer.

sed Rewrites the Stream

Normalizing before parsing
$ sed 's/[0-9]\{1,3\}\(\.[0-9]\{1,3\}\)\{3\}/IP/g' auth.log# mask every address
09:14:22 sshd Failed password for root from IP port 51022
Why it mattersStructuring data means deciding what counts as the same thing. That decision starts here, not in the model.

The Detectors We Will Train

  • Classical baselines first, so you have something to beat.
  • Sequence models on the time-series set: LSTMs, windows, and leakage.
  • Transformers, and parameter-efficient tuning of language models on log text.
  • Reinforcement learning where the response, not just the label, is the decision.
NoteA baseline you can beat is worth more than a large model you cannot explain.
