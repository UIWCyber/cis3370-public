Yellow Is the Right Answer

  • Cluster health has three colours and only one of them is a problem.
  1. 1Green: every shard is where it should be, including copies.
  2. 2Yellow: your data is all there and searchable, but a copy is missing.
  3. 3Red: some data is not available. This is the one that matters.
Why it mattersYou are running one node. A copy of a shard has to live on a different node, so a single node can never place one, and any index that asks for a replica sits at yellow forever. Right now that is five unassigned shards, one per index, and nothing is wrong.

A Mapping Is the dtypes You Declare in Advance

  • pandas guesses the type when it reads the file. Elasticsearch decides when the first document arrives, and then it is fixed.
DefinitionMapping. The declared type of every field in an index. Written before the data lands, or inferred from the first document that carries the field.
Watch outChange your mind later and you do not alter the index, you build a new one and reindex into it. Deciding the mapping is cheap now and expensive in three weeks.

Why Every Text Field Has a Twin

  • A field like service arrives as two things: service, which is analyzed for searching, and service.keyword, which is the exact string.
  1. 1Search service when you want "find the word http anywhere in this field".
  2. 2Use service.keyword when you want "group by the exact value" or "sort".
  3. 3Aggregate on the analyzed field and Elasticsearch refuses, because the words were taken apart.
Analogyservice is the book's index at the back, one entry per word. service.keyword is the title on the spine, kept whole.

The Trap Everyone Falls Into Once

  • Discover opens with a time range of the last fifteen minutes. The capture in the bundle is years old.
  1. 1You will see "No results match your search criteria" and believe the load failed.
  2. 2The load did not fail. You are looking at the wrong fifteen minutes of history.
  3. 3Set the range to cover the capture, then the documents appear.
Watch outEvery time-series tool defaults to now. When a query returns nothing, check the clock before you check the data.

Aggregations Answer Over Everything

  • The point is not speed on 6,435 documents. It is that the same query works on a million, without loading them.

  2. 2Ask for a count per minute and you get the histogram Discover is already drawing.
  3. 3Ask across five indices at once and it is the same call with a comma.

Why It Can Hold More Than Your Laptop

  • An index is cut into shards, and a shard is a piece that fits on one machine.
  1. 1More data than one machine holds: add machines, add shards.
  2. 2A machine dies: a replica shard on another machine still has the data.
  3. 3One node, as you have today: shards exist, replicas cannot, hence yellow.
AnalogyA shard is a filing cabinet drawer. One drawer per room is fine until the paperwork outgrows the room, and then you add rooms rather than bigger drawers.

Delete It and Start Again, on Purpose

  • The stack is disposable, and this course disposes of it regularly.
  1. 1down stops the containers and keeps the data.
  2. 2down -v removes the volume, which is every document you loaded.
  3. 3A cluster built from an empty volume gets a fresh 30-day trial license, which is why the class redeploys.
Watch outThe trial gives you machine learning, the security app and graph analytics. When it expires the cluster stops answering until you switch to the free license, and those three are gone. That is a licensing fact, not a bug, and it is why you will do this setup more than once.
