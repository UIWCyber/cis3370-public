NSL-KDD: every column, verified against the files

The feature table that circulates with this dataset has mistakes in it. Every range and value set below was recomputed from the files you download, over 148,517 rows of KDDTrain+.txt and KDDTest+.txt together. Where the table and the data disagreed, the data won.

How to read this page

Names are exactly what the @attribute lines in KDDTrain+.arff call them, which is why they are lower case: you import them rather than typing them. Scale is the measurement scale from the lecture: nominal means the numbers are labels, ratio means they are quantities with a real zero. Kind is a separate question: discrete counts against continuous amounts. Range is what the two files actually contain, not what any table claims. Amber rows are the 5 places the circulating table is wrong.

Two things to carry into your own analysis

A count is discrete even when a table calls it continuous. You cannot have 2.5 root accesses. The circulating table labels columns such as hot and num_root "continuous integers", which is not a thing. They are ratio scale and discrete.

Whole numbers are not automatically counts. duration, src_bytes and dst_bytes are stored as whole numbers, but they measure how long and how much, not how many. They are the continuous columns in this dataset, and they are the ones with the long tails that need a transform.

The 15 rate columns are proportions. They run 0.00 to 1.00 in steps of 0.01, so they look discrete if you count distinct values. Treat them as continuous proportions, and remember they are already normalised: scaling them again does nothing.
