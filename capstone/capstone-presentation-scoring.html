Live reproduction

40% Score / 40
ExcellentThe repository runs from a clean checkout in front of the room and reproduces at least one number from the comparison table, live. The team drives it confidently and knows what each step is doing.
AdequateThe pipeline runs, live or via the required recording, and produces a number, but part of it is described rather than shown, or the run starts from saved intermediate files rather than a clean checkout.
Missing or weakNothing is run live or recorded, or what runs does not produce any number that appears in the report.

The reproduction, told honestly

30% Score / 30
ExcellentThe claim, the data, the pipeline and the comparison table are all clear, and the team names every choice the paper left unstated and what it did about each. A listener could go and repeat the work.
AdequateThe claim and the result are covered and compared, but the unstated choices are glossed over, or the baseline is missing, or the pipeline walkthrough is too thin to repeat.
Missing or weakThere is no side-by-side comparison with the paper, or the claim being reproduced is never stated in one sentence, or a mismatch is presented as a success.

Professionalism and Q&A

30% Score / 30
ExcellentPracticed and in time (10 to 15 minutes), every member speaks to their user stories, and the team shows mastery answering questions.
AdequateEvery member speaks but parts are uneven, timing slips, or some answers wander.
Missing or weakA member does not present, the talk runs well over or under time, or questions go unanswered.
Missed slot deduction: failing to present in the scheduled slot is an automatic 15% deduction from the team's total capstone grade. Record it here and apply it on the grading rubric, not in the scores above.
