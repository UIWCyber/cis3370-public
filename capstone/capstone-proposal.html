3. The Paper and the Claim

Name the paper you will reproduce and state, precisely, the result you are going to try to obtain. A topic is not a claim. A number or a table is.

4. Feasibility Check

Four questions decide whether this paper can be reproduced at all: can you get the data, is the method described in enough detail to rebuild, are the metrics defined precisely enough to recompute, and does the compute fit the hardware and time you have? Add one row per question with the evidence for your answer.

5. Data Plan

Where the data comes from and what state it is in. You must already be able to load it before this proposal is approved.

6. Reproduction Plan

The pipeline you will rebuild, stage by stage, and where you expect the paper to be underspecified.

7. Evaluation and Comparison Plan

How you will decide whether you reproduced the claim. Agree this before you have results, so the answer is not chosen to suit them.

8. Resources Required
